fix(panel): make the floating panel resizable and always restorable

Two reported usability defects in the injected panel:

1. The panel could not be resized. It was pinned at a fixed 580x340 with no
   size control. Added a drag grip in the top-left corner (the panel is
   anchored bottom-right, so dragging left/up grows it), clamped to
   320px..viewport and 160px..viewport, with an explicit height and
   `.awt-out { flex: 1 1 auto }` so the output area follows the new size.

2. Clicking collapse made the panel unrecoverable. The collapsed rule
   shrank the panel to 260px while the header still carried every control,
   so `overflow: hidden` clipped the collapse button itself. Fixed by
   pinning `.awt-collapse` absolutely at right/top with z-index above the
   header, wrapping the header, hiding the unused controls while collapsed,
   and routing every toggle through `setCollapsed()` which saves/restores
   the inline width/height and swaps the label between `-` and `+`.
   Double-clicking the title is a second restore path.

Tests: added a jsdom case asserting the grip exists, collapse hides the
output area and shows `+`, and a second click restores `-`. Suite is now
58 tests in 10 files, all passing. Docs updated (test counts, known
issues, README usage step).
